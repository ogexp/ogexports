The OG Exports investor mobile app should use a premium white-and-emerald visual language: white and soft off-white surfaces, dark emerald hierarchy and primary actions, fresh green for restrained highlights, and gold only as a small accent. Keep the app in light appearance rather than switching to a dark palette based on device settings.

Use spacious typography, rounded white cards with subtle borders and shadows, and white secondary actions with emerald borders. Avoid crypto, gambling, or speculative-return visual cues. Decorative portfolio illustrations must not imply actual performance or introduce financial amounts, forecasts, or other unreported data.

**Why:** the user specified this visual direction for investor trust, and this product must not suggest financial claims unsupported by its API.

**How to apply:** use the mobile app's semantic theme tokens across screens, keep gold rare, and use only API-provided financial information in data-bearing visuals.
