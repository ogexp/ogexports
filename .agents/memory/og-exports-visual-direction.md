name: OG Exports mobile visual direction
description: The requested premium light brand style and data-safe visual constraints for the investor app.

The OG Exports investor mobile app should use a premium emerald visual language: white and soft off-white surfaces, dark emerald hierarchy and primary actions, fresh green for restrained highlights, and gold only as a small accent. Light is the default appearance; users can choose Light, Dark, or System.

Use four bottom tabs: Home, Explore, Portfolio, and Wallet. Keep Profile out of the tab bar and make it accessible from the top-right avatar/menu.

The metallic emerald “OG” artwork supplied by the user is the official logo. Use that artwork for app identity instead of the previous sprout mark, with a transparent background and its original emerald colors.

Use spacious typography, rounded white cards with subtle borders and shadows, and white secondary actions with emerald borders. Avoid crypto, gambling, or speculative-return visual cues. Decorative portfolio illustrations must not imply actual performance or introduce financial amounts, forecasts, or other unreported data.

**Why:** the master prompt specifies the four-tab navigation and top-right Profile access, alongside the premium visual direction and official logo. Phase 27 explicitly adds Light, Dark, and System appearance choices, superseding the earlier light-only direction.

**How to apply:** use the transparent OG artwork for app icon and in-app branding without a dark backing shape, use semantic theme tokens across screens and system bars, keep gold rare, and preserve the four-tab navigation with Profile in the avatar/menu.
