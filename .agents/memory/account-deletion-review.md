---
name: Account deletion review
description: OG Exports policy for investor account and personal-data deletion requests.
---

Account and associated-data deletion requests use verified Support review rather than immediate erasure. Support confirms account ownership, reviews active investments and legal or contractual retention requirements, then deletes or anonymizes data that is eligible for removal. Explain any records that must remain.

**Why:** OG Exports chose this process because investor, transaction, and compliance records may need to be preserved for valid obligations.

**How to apply:** Keep the in-app and public web request paths clear that submitting a request does not immediately delete or disable the account. Do not claim completion until Support finishes the review.
