BRAND THEME

The complete application must follow a Premium White + Emerald Green visual identity.

Primary Colors

* Premium White: #FFFFFF
* Off White: #F7F7F2
* Dark Emerald Green: #0F3D2E

* Charcoal: #1F2933
* Light Green: #EAF4EF

The overall feeling should be:

Trustworthy • Premium • Secure • Global • Professional
