OG EXPORTS — PREMIUM TRADE INVESTMENT MOBILE APP

MASTER DEVELOPMENT PROMPT — PHASE-WISE

Build a production-ready, premium, modern, luxury-level mobile investment platform for OG Exports (Private) Limited, focused exclusively on Import/Export Container Investment Opportunities.

This must NOT look like a generic finance app, crypto app, stock trading app, or ordinary business app.

The product identity should be:

Real-World Trade. Digital Investment. Transparent Tracking.

The platform allows eligible investors to discover specific import/export trade opportunities, invest in individual container-based trades, monitor trade/container progress, manage their wallet, view portfolio performance, access documents, and receive trade/settlement updates.
